Be Prepared

Documents to Ask About

The following are commonly requested for college admissions. The exact requirements may vary.

  • Previous examination marks sheet
  • Matriculation certificate, if required
  • Student's B-Form or other accepted identification
  • Father/guardian's CNIC copy, if required
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • College admission form
  • Migration or school-leaving certificate, if applicable
