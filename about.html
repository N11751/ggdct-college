WHO WE ARE

Education That Inspires Success

Welcome to G.G.D.C.T. College. This website provides information about our college, intermediate academic programs and student learning resources.

Our academic focus includes First Year (Class XI) and Second Year (Class XII), with Pre-Medical and Pre-Engineering programs.

Students can explore the syllabus, educational videos, college activities and other academic information through this website.
