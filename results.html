How to Check Your Result

  1. Open the relevant examination board website.
  2. Find the Intermediate results section.
  3. Select the required examination and year.
  4. Enter your roll number if requested.
  5. Follow the board's instructions to view your result.

Note: Results are not stored on this college website. The button opens the board website; availability of a particular result depends on the board.
